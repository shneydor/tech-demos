# 02: Take a waiting Article back, or archive it

**What to build:** From the Snoozed list, the reader can unsnooze a waiting Article or archive it. Unsnooze puts it back in the ordinary Inbox immediately, at its original save time, with no return time. Archive from that list sets it to archived, clears the wake instant, and keeps it out of the Inbox, so it does not come back at 05:00.

**Blocked by:** 01. Snooze an Article onto the Snoozed list

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Unsnooze from the Snoozed list puts the Article back in the Inbox immediately.
- [ ] The unsnoozed Article keeps its original save time, has no return time, and joins the ordinary Inbox rather than the clock-returned top.
- [ ] Unsnooze is the only action that puts an Article back at its original save time.
- [ ] Archive from the Snoozed list sets the status to archived and clears the wake instant.
- [ ] An Article archived from the Snoozed list stays out of the Inbox and does not come back at 05:00.
