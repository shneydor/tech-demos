# Huashu Motion

## Goal

A visitor opens the gallery card and presses play. The featured film is a real huashu-motion launch film for Automat-it, made with the tool's scoring loop from public facts on automat-it.com. A switch chooses the published Tea Across the Ages showcase instead. On the Automat-it film, Walk the edit replays one comment that was actually applied: `om inbox`, `om look` (before), the edit, and `om done`, with the live player showing the film after that edit. The page says what was generated by the tool and what is a replay. No server, no key, no agent in the browser.

## In scope

- Featured film: Automat-it, 1920×1080, about 17 s, played live through `<om-player>` with the soundtrack `om sound` wrote. Switcher: Automat-it (default) and Tea Across the Ages.
- Facts only from the public homepage fetched on 2026-11-28, plus the logo and colours `om grab https://www.automat-it.com` saved. No invented metrics, no customer logos.
- Scoring loop as the tool defines it for a launch film: an `om-score` in ABC, `OM.music({ bpm })` with scene labels on `OM.beat`, `om sound`, and every `warning score:` line fixed. Narration: none. Instruments: synths, because `om samples` is not installed. The page says so.
- Gates that can be run here: `om check` with 0 errors, `om still` of the key frames, `om render`, `om qa`. The three direction cards are written in `treatment.md`. This run does not wait for a style reply; the task already asked for the film, and the chosen direction is recorded there.
- One real edit on the closing line. `addComment` (the studio's writer), `om inbox --wait`, `om look` before the change, `om set` on the tagline variables only, `om done --note`. The live film is the after state. The walk shows the before still, the after still, the inbox printout, and the done note.
- Tea Across the Ages stays the published showcase, with its earlier recorded comment. That walk still says `om done` was not run and that the showcase has no soundtrack.
- `bun run build` copies the committed exports. It does not clone the tool or launch Chrome. GitHub Pages has neither.

## Out of scope

- Re-running the director loop on every Pages build.
- A live agent, a comment box, or `om studio` in the demo.
- Customer logos, quotes presented as logos, or numbers that are not on the fetched homepage.
- A paid music or voice API. None is required, and none is used.
- Changes outside `apps/huashu-motion/` and the existing `tracking/seen-bookmarks.json` entry.

## How the Automat-it film is made

Tool: `node /tmp/huashu-motion/cli/om.js` at v0.6.1.

```bash
export OM_CHROME=/tmp/chrome-nosandbox
om grab https://www.automat-it.com /tmp/automat
om learn launch rhythm sound
om new /tmp/ait
# treatment.md, then index.html (logo file, grabbed colours, homepage sentences)
om sound /tmp/ait          # repeat until no warning score:
om check /tmp/ait
om still /tmp/ait --at <hook,burst,stop,end>
om render /tmp/ait
om qa /tmp/ait
# comment on the closing line, then:
om inbox /tmp/ait --wait
om look <id> /tmp/ait      # before
om set taglineSize=… taglineTop=… taglineWeight=… taglineColor=… /tmp/ait
om done <id> /tmp/ait --note "…"
om still /tmp/ait --at <same t>   # after
om export html /tmp/ait --out /tmp/ait-share --title "Automat-it"
```

`om sound` writes `assets/sound.wav` and `<audio id="om-sound">`. The browser does not synthesize the score; the player plays that file. The page says the score was performed by `om sound` on synths.

Direction chosen: kinetic type on the homepage's own sentence and figures. The other two cards in the treatment are a photo cut of the grabbed screenshots (the hero shot includes a cookie dialog, so it is not the film) and a depth stack of the three practices (included because the method asks for one depth option; not used, because the site has no product object to put in space).

The opening shot scales `#s-hook` from 1.42 to 1 over 2.1s with `transform-origin: 0% 46%`, so the wordmark stays inside 1920×1080. An earlier center-origin push cropped the logo. The committed export is the re-render after that fix. `om qa` on that render has no ✗. `om check` reports 0 errors and the `only-logo` warning, which stays: the film is type, and the grabbed screenshots include a cookie dialog.

## UX

- First screen: Automat-it at frame 0, Play, and a switcher. Play starts the selected film from the beginning. Switching films resets the walk and seeks to 0.
- Scene strip from that film's live `scene:` labels.
- Automat-it banner: the picture and the soundtrack were generated by huashu-motion 0.6.1; the walk is a replay of comment `<id>`, including the `om done` note. Tea banner: unchanged (published film, recorded comment, `om done` not run, no soundtrack).
- Walk steps for Automat-it: the comment, the pin on `#tagline`, the note, the inbox printout, then before and after stills of that spot. Tea keeps its five steps and its look stills.
- Error: the player's own message if the film never becomes ready. A missing still says so.

   - Acceptance: `om sound` prints no `warning score:` line; `assets/sound.wav` exists; scene labels sit on `OM.beat`.
   - Dependencies: this plan.
2. `om check`, stills, `om render`, `om qa`. Fix anything those commands mark as an error.
   - Acceptance: `om check` reports 0 errors; a render exists; `om qa` has been read and its failures fixed or recorded if the tool's own threshold cannot be met without inventing footage.
   - Dependencies: task 1.
3. One comment through `om done`, with before and after stills.
   - Acceptance: inbox text names the comment id, the time, `#tagline`, and a source line; `om done` is in the comment record; the after still shows the line higher and heavier than the before still; nothing else in the copy changes.
   - Dependencies: task 2.
4. Switcher on the page, Automat-it featured.
   - Acceptance: the built page opens on Automat-it; Play moves its clock and the picture; the other switch loads Tea; the Automat-it walk shows before and after; the Tea walk still says `om done` was not run.
   - Dependencies: task 3.
5. Play the build under `/tech-demos/huashu-motion/`.
   - Acceptance: both films reach ready; the Automat-it soundtrack element is present; the subpath loads the engine.
   - Dependencies: task 4.

## Risks

- Pages cannot run Chrome or the scoring loop. Mitigation: commit the export, the wav, and the stills. `bun run build` only bundles them. The page says the tool ran once, at build time, on this machine.
- A visitor can think the browser composed the music. Mitigation: the banner says `om sound` wrote the wav and the player only plays it.
- Grabbed screenshots include a cookie dialog and partner marks that are not Automat-it's logo. Mitigation: the film uses the wordmark SVG `om grab` saved (`logo-05.svg`) and homepage sentences. It does not paste those screenshots or other companies' logos.
- The tagline edit could fail contrast or `om check`. Mitigation: re-run `om check` after `om set` and fix before exporting.
- Tea's "the film was not rewritten" copy must not appear on the Automat-it walk. Mitigation: each film has its own banner.
